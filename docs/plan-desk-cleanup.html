A Desk that earns its screen

Every one of the 13 pages was rendered from today's book (v1497) and read line by line. The Desk is sound but crowded: 11 faults, about a dozen sections that repeat another page or measure nothing, and almost no control that answers "what if". This plan fixes the faults, cuts the repeats and adds three planning tools. One version, one commit a part.

The answer

Three parts, in this order

  1. Fix. Eleven faults found on the live book, each with a check proved red.
  2. Cut. Sections that repeat another page, or that say "not measured", go. Nothing that prices, books or holds money is touched.
  3. Plan. Three what-if tools placed where the forecast already sits. They read the book, keep their inputs on the device, and price or book nothing.

Left alone: the Pricing page (the Price Band build is reworking it in another session) and every PrEP line (the Removing PrEP session owns those).

Part 3

Planning tools

Each reads the book and the engine. Inputs stay on the device and are marked "what if"; nothing is priced, queued or booked.
Restock planner · Stock, Next 30 days

Add a lot you are thinking of (book, units, day, rate). The inventory and cash charts redraw with it: the day each book runs empty, the cash low point, and what the lot ties up. Remove it and the forecast is the book's again.

Cash on hand · Money, Financials

Type the cash you hold today. The Desk can then answer what it now cannot: whether the next lot is affordable, and the lowest the cash goes in the next 30 days.

Month ahead · Money, Financials

Three steppers: demand (-50% to +50%), buying cost a unit, and monthly running costs (replacing the "not measured" lines). It shows next month's revenue, margin and what is left after costs, and how many orders break even.
