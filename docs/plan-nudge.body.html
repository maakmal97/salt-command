Why it can be built now what stopped it on 17 Nov is gone

The first attempt stopped on one fault. During a deploy an old Desk and a new Desk could each queue a different part of one payment, MYR 150 on the book for MYR 100 paid. No safe order of deploys was found.

v1542 took that road away today. The minute's pass books no payment any more. Money reaches the ledger only by your Received or your Cash received, and each checks what the row can still take.

So the attempt's heavy parts are not needed: no payment claim on the Counter, no reserved place in the queue. What is left is small: one call, one route, and one guard so a burst of moves runs one pass and then one more.

How it works one call, no figure in it

  1. 1. The Counter records the moveEvery customer's move is written in one place, the order book. Right after the write, where it already tells the open pages, it also tells the Desk.
  2. 2. The call says only that something movedAn empty POST over the link the two Workers already share, behind the key they already share. No figure, name, order or word travels. The customer's tap never waits for it.
  3. 3. The Desk answers at once and runs the pass behindThe same steps as the minute, in the same order: read the Counter, ring your banner, queue the rows, spend a yes you gave in advance, draft for Approve.
  4. 4. A burst runs one pass, then one moreOn both sides: while a call or a pass is running, another only leaves a mark, and one more runs when the first ends. Ten moves in a second are two passes, not ten.
  5. 5. The minute still runsA call that is lost, a deploy in the middle of a move, a Desk that is busy: the every-minute pass catches it within a minute, as today.

Verified One such call already exists on master for one move: their Yes to your offer tells the Desk at once and does not wait for the minute.

The deploy. The Desk goes first, as the Actions job already does. For the few minutes an old Desk meets a new Counter, the call finds no route, is dropped in silence, and the minute's pass does the work. Either way round, nothing is booked twice and nothing is lost.

The checks. Fable reviews the change as a money path. Each new check is proved by breaking it. The full suite runs, then one version is pushed and proved live.

The parts one version, one commit a part
