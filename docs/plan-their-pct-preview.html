The curve, as you set it

Your answer of 22 Oct 2026: three curves of the same shape across lot size. The Set Price at 0%, your low ends at -30% and your high ends at +30%. Each 5% step is an equal share of the way from the Set Price to the end curve, at every size.

  • -30%: 2.00x at 0.5 unit, 1.20x at 12.5.
  • Set Price: 2.50x at 0.5 unit, 1.33x at 12.5 (live today; you wrote 1.35, see Ask 1).
  • +30%: 3.00x at 0.5 unit (the Ceiling), 1.45x at 12.5.
  • Between the ends: each end curve keeps the Set Price's own shape. Wherever the Set Price sits between its two ends, the -30% and +30% curves sit at the same share between theirs. Above 12.5 units everything is flat.

Data path

  1. Engine: engine/pricing.mjs gains driftMultiple(q, R, pct). The walk reads it in place of the multiple times (1 + %). The ends live in salt's TIER_RULE as ends: {low: [2.00, 1.20], high: [3.00, 1.45]}, at the first and last points. A rule without ends keeps today's law, so nothing else moves. It is synced into the master's ENGINE block, and the Desk's Rules page wording follows.
  2. Desk read: makerDriftPreview(book, code, product, pct) in src/maker-inputs.js, priced by the engine's quoteAt and floorTotal over the pricing snapshot. It refuses a code not on the roster, a bucket, a supplier, a partner, a book without a Set Price, and a % that driftOk refuses. Route: GET /_internal/maker/drift-preview/<code>/<product>/<pct> in src/worker.js, over DESK_MAKER with COUNTER_MAKER_KEY only.
  3. Counter relay: GET /all/maker/drift-preview/... in stmt/maker.js, behind Access and the owner email.
  4. Salt Admin: stmt/owner.js. Waits 300 ms after typing, drops a reply that arrives after a newer one, keeps replies so going back to a figure is instant, and uses the design system's table recipe.
