3 · The plan

The basket, end to end

Item 12. His answers: taps on one mark sum into one order at the summed amount's own list price; at five open every + greys with "Five open orders is the most"; nothing is refused at Place; a basket is one sheet and counts once.

State, page memory only

  • draft.bk {product: amount}: the basket's lines, one a product, normalised as the any-amount field does (page.js 3978).
  • draft.bkTaps {product: {q: n}}: the tally for the badges and "10 units (5 + 5)", kept only while it adds up to the line; otherwise dropped, the pane reads "{amount} in your order" and its minus takes the product out whole.
  • Lifetime: lost on reload, cleared at sign-out; nothing is stored on the device.

Controls on Prices (P4)

  • .szrow unchanged: it opens the sheet at that size alone and leaves the basket untouched.
  • + beside it (salt-orb, glyphs plus and minus), data-add holding the size only; no aria-label, a hidden span with pshape(product) and the size, as page.js 5799 does.
  • Enabled only when priceAt(P, bk[p] + q) prices it (salt steps 0.25, rice and candy 1, oil 10), openN() < OMAX, and not his view, hold or hidePrices.
  • Minus never refuses: a remainder priceAt cannot price shows e.offStep and greys Review.
  • A tap patches the badge and the bar only, never drawPrices, so focus stays; openN is re-read after each loadOrders.

Bar, Review, Place, Sent (P5)

  1. Bar. Line count, the goods total (the sum of priceAt over the lines, as formFoot adds them) and Review; "and delivery, set when we confirm it" when wayNow() is deliver.
  2. Review. Copies only priced lines into draft.pick (never oCanPick), sets draft.src='basket', opens the sheet, then the check when formWhy() is empty; greyed while a line has no price.
  3. In the sheet. A change writes back to the basket; Remove, only while nothing is placed, takes the product out and freezes the check again with fresh rids.
  4. Place. The existing oPlaceIt: a body a line, its own rid, one sheet id for two or more, the digest; the Worker's checkPlacement (orders.js 553 to 619) already takes it.
  5. Sent. At page.js 4232, before the check is cleared, placed products leave draft.pick on every road (his answer 3) and the basket when src is basket; dropPlaced does the same on Change and Close; the bar is patched after.
  6. Edges. A partly placed check keeps its placed line marked; reviewed again it mints a new sheet id, as today. A new list keeps unpriced lines with their hint. Under hold or hidePrices nothing is drawn and the basket waits.

Order again's road into Place

Item 4. Only on orders handed over in full whose size is still on their list; none on Cancelled or Declined.
  1. Who gets it. oAgainOk(o): movedAll(o) (4494), not cancelled or declined, the size on the list, not a group order, and not his view, hold or hidePrices; Home's tiles take the same predicate. A read-only count of done orders with moved 0 comes first.
  2. Order screen. A new OPARTS part again: one Pill and "{rm} today, same size and place", read off the list now, never o.total.
  3. Earlier row. A wrapper carrying data-row holds the card and a sibling ghost; oSync reads closest('[data-row]') (4948) so a poll keeps focus.
  4. Tap. againOpen sets way, place words and who-for, and resets when, wantBy, slot and src, so no stale day or window rides into the check.
  5. Check and Place. The existing sheet: one line at today's figure, a fresh rid, no sheet id, no note, the digest; the limit step at five open.
