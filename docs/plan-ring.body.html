What changes one version, one commit a part

  1. 1. Every device at onceA banner is sent to all your devices together, and to a customer's phones the same way. A send still open after 3 s is let go and counted as failed.
  2. 2. The Counter rings your Salt Admin at the moveThe moment the order book records a customer's move, it rings Salt Admin for it, by the Desk's own rule: a new order, their I have sent it, a withdrawal, a rating, their Yes or No thanks, a line. A tap on the banner opens that order.
  3. 3. The Desk rings only what was not rungThe Desk reads what the Counter rang and sends nothing for it. Anything the Counter could not ring, the Desk rings as before. Its own marks are read and written together, and its banner step runs beside the queueing, not before it.
  4. 4. The pass works on the order that movedThe call names the orders moved, ids alone. The Desk's pass queues those, and every order owing its pending row, and leaves the orders that wait for their rows to the minute.
  5. 5. The queue reads togetherQueueing a row reads its three places at once, deciding in the same order as before.
  6. 6. An open Salt Admin hears a new rowA drafted row was told to Salt Desk's pages alone, so Salt Admin's To approve waited for its poll. It is now told at once, a kind and nothing else, never to a customer.

The guards on your banner from Fable's review of 19 Nov 2026

Two places can now ring you, so each guard stops a second banner or a lost one.

  • The Counter rings only what that move did, never older news, so the first move after the deploy rings only itself.
  • It records a kind as rung only when one of your devices took it, and never moves that record back when a slow ring finishes late.
  • While a ring is on its way, the Desk neither rings nor moves its own mark for it; the move's own call, or the minute, reads it again once the ring has ended.
  • The Counter tells the Desk only after its ring, so the Desk's pass reads what was rung.
  • A ring that fails records nothing, and the Desk rings as it did before.

What it leaves alone

The minute's pass still runs everything, as the net. No price, row or rule changes. The call between the two Workers still carries no figure, name or word, only order ids. With no device subscribed to Salt Admin, Salt Desk still rings its own banner.

The Desk deploys before the Counter, as on every run, so an old Counter only ever meets a new Desk, which rings as before.
