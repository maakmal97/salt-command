3 · The plan

Saved spots, end to end

Item 18. His answer: kept on this phone only; label and floor or unit ride inside the sealed salt-pin with the point; nothing new on the Counter; a new phone starts with none.

On the phone (P5)

  • Store: localStorage salt-spots:<username>, at most three {id, label, place, unit, pt}: label up to 24 characters (Shop, Store, Warehouse or typed), place the area words under draft.place's own rule (60), unit up to 40 and optional, pt optional and one of today's three point shapes. Read and written in try, as salt-look is; a store that throws leaves the sheet as today.
  • Per account: keyed by username, so a phone holding a kept second account shows each its own. The test shim's salt-test: namespace keeps /all/test's spots apart.
  • Lifetime: until Remove, or Log out of this phone (logOut, page.js 2907): the signed-in account's key is dropped whether or not it is the kept one (2914 clears the others only when !other). Sign out everywhere cannot reach a phone; the next open there finds them until that phone logs out (Ask 3).
  • Save on this phone off: a one-off spot, used for this order and stored nowhere.
  • At three: Add a spot gives way to sp.full, "Three saved. Remove one to add another." Remove asks no confirm.

The sheet

  • Where to lists the spots as salt-option__face--tap radios, each with an Edit ghost (named "Edit Shop", 44px) and Add a spot. Picking one sets draft.place = spot.place, draft.spot = spot.id and the seal input; today's free-text area stays inside Add a spot as Area.
  • Default: the spot this phone used last (salt-spots keeps its order), else none picked (Ask 4).
  • Next waits for a spot when delivering, as it waits for an area today (sh.whyWhere, 4166). The gate's pin pass becomes draft.pin && draft.pin.src !== 'unit', so a unit alone never stands in for the area words.
  • Amend before Accept cannot carry a pin: page.js 4401 sends none, stmt/orders.js 732 to 735 refuses a pin key (amendShape) and the amend route runs no takePin. So Change order shows the spot as kept (draft.pinKept) and hides the picker and the unit; decideReplace (737) re-attaches the original pin (Ask 7).

The seal (P6)

  • Payload pinSeal (page.js 4251): today {v:1, at, ...point}. Now {v:1, at, label?, unit?, ...point}, and when a unit is typed with no exact spot, the new shape {v:1, at, label?, unit, src:'unit'}. A spot with neither unit nor point seals nothing, as today.
  • Same protocol and info salt-pin v1: the Desk's reader ignores fields it does not know, so the version stays v1 (Ask 5).
  • Caps on the seal input, one-off spots included, so a long unit is cut on the phone, never refused as pinBad after the sheet said yes. Size: label 24 and unit 40 add well under 200 characters of base64; PIN_MAX 4096 holds. The Worker is unchanged: takePin, keepPin, the test-account drop (stmt/worker.js 489).
  • The order record keeps the pin id; the customer reads pinned as a neutral row, od.sealed "Spot or unit: Sealed" (Ask 6). The area words travel in the clear as today; the label never leaves the phone unsealed.

The Desk (P7)

  • ordPinPoint carries label and unit out with the point into ORD_PIN[k] (19395), dropped by lockVault as today; ordPinLine (19398) reads "Shop · Unit 12A" before the point's line, after the one unlock.
  • A unit alone reads "A unit, no exact spot" instead of "holds no point"; pinFileable (19424) is false, so File all's count (19431 to 19445) skips it, and the fee falls back to the filed place as today.
  • Filing (ordPinFile, 19413) files the point alone, {lat, lng, src:'pin'}, into vault-addr; the unit and label stay on the order (Ask 8).
  • Salt Admin (stmt/owner.js 4034) says "They shared a spot or unit. Open it on the Desk."
4 · The plan

The confirm time, measured

Item 19. His answer: median Place to Accept over his last 30 confirmations made in Counter hours, rounded up to the next quarter hour; only with 10 or more; only while open.

The measure (P3, stmt/confirm.js)

  • confirmWithin(orders, cfg), pure: placed is order.at (stmt/orders.js 717); accepted is the first history line with status acknowledged (folded at 1140 to 1155).
  • In Counter hours: both moments inside hoursStatus({schedule: cfg.schedule, manual: null}, t).open (stmt/hours.js 102), each moment passed as a number (Date.parse; order.at and history[].at are ISO strings, and openAt adds to its argument, hours.js 19), judged on the weekly hours as they stand now; the manual override is left out, since it would recolour past moments (108) (Ask 9).
  • Left out: an Accept sent to them as an offer (price.from === "accept", 540 to 567), a group order closed by the Maker, the test accounts, a withdrawn order never accepted, and any order with a price (an ask haggled before Accept, stmt/orders.js 1209 to 1212, as well as an Accept sent as an offer). A replacement is measured from the root of its chain (replaces, 740, walked back, stopping on a missing root or a repeat and falling back to the order's own at), so the read takes cancelled orders too (allOrders(env, true), the read toChase already makes hourly).
  • The figure: the 30 latest by accept moment; none under 10; the median (an even count takes the mean of the middle two); up to the next 15 minutes, never under 15. Returns {mins, n} or null.

Where it is computed and how it travels

  • The Counter's hourly cron (stmt/worker.js scheduled, 2066 to 2110) reads the order book once and writes KV confirm:within {mins, n, at}, in its own try BEFORE the chase's slot return (2077), so it runs every hour, overnight included, and a bad schedule (hoursStatus throws, 104) never skips the chase.
  • On GET /hours (stmt/worker.js 1674): publicHours (stmt/maker.js 741) adds confirm, dropped when at is over three hours old, so a stuck cron shows nothing rather than an old figure. The page already polls it; nothing is sealed, because the figure is site-wide and moves without a publish (Ask 10).

On the placed order (P4)

  • Open (counterHours.open === true) with confirm.n >= 10: ow.within "Usually confirmed within {d}" and ow.measured "Measured from our last {n} orders, not a promise." {d} reads "45 minutes", "1 hour", "1 hour 15 minutes" (Ask 11).
  • Closed: ow.closed "We confirm when the Counter opens" and ow.opens "Opens at {hh:mm}, {Ddd DD Mon}." from nextOpen, no figure.
  • Open with under 10, or hours unknown (hoursError, or before the first read): today's ow.placed, no figure.
  • Only on a placed order that waits on his Accept; an amended order back at Placed shows it again.
