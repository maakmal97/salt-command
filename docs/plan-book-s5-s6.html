The Book becomes the source: eight parts, the flip last

The tap is on and has booked its first approvals. S5 makes the Book the source of the ledger and S6 joins it to the order book. Fable reviewed the design and found that three things the old plan skipped would cost money at the flip: nothing books an Add ID, a rename or a username once the fold stops; the first dispatch after the flip would fold every tapped draft a second time; and a code deploy would silently make every reader fall back to a stale copy. The plan below puts those first, keeps every part dormant until the flip, and starts the 7 days of evidence now.

The gate before the flip

Every line is read off the chain's load line and the minute net's log over the 7 days.

  • Every load: tap: N closed, 0 open, and no difference, stuck or clash.
  • At least 20 taps closed, 5 of them new rows with a minted ID, and one trip, one purchase lot and one Received.
  • The readcheck green at every load; the net's left at 0 and voided only after a rejection.
  • Zero approvals that fell back: a fallback means the Book refused a rule the fold would take.
  • The Worker's PRICING equal to the page's at every load, including after a lot.
  • The export's replay equal to git's book at every fold; one Book backup opened with your key.
  • No drafts.mjs --approve run in the window.

The flip and its way back

  1. Wait out Actions and approved rows, fold once more, read 0 open.
  2. Deploy the flag-reading code with the flag off: no re-seed of the mirror, no dispatch for booked kinds, a load refused without an import, the laptop roads refused.
  3. Run the export once and compact.
  4. Mark every booked draft committed in one statement and read it back.
  5. Set the source flag to the Book. Watch one tap end to end.
Way back, 60 to 90 minutesRun the export, set the flag back to D1, seed the mirror from the export, hand-fold what was tapped since, re-enable the stage. It holds only if the export ran; otherwise add a hand fold.

Asks for you

  • A. A hand edit after the flip (marking a refund paid, removing a stale refund, a hand fold). Recommended: an edit entry you approve in Salt Admin for the common ones, and a load of the export from the laptop for the rare ones. The other way is the import alone.
  • B. The laptop roads. Recommended: drafts.mjs --approve and update.mjs refuse once the flip flag is on, because after the flip each would be a second ledger.
  • C. How soon a note follows its row. Recommended: once a day, with the export job (the notes call runs where the model key lives). A row shows at once; its note can be up to a day behind. The other way is hourly.
