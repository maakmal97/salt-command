How each move is proved

A check that names the old system is re-aimed at the new part with a dated note, never deleted to go green, and every new check first fails by its own mutation. The carried copies of Ambient are checked against the lock as the page serves them. Each place is drawn at 390, 860, 1080, 1180 and 1440 pixels, in light and in dark, and measured: no text under 12px, no control under 44px, no field under 16px, nothing cut, spilling or scrolling sideways.

A fresh Opus reviewer reviews the design from those shots before the version ships, and Fable reviews it once its limit lifts. A version is live when the chain is green and the page serves its build.
