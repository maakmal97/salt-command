The Counter is fast; the Desk on a phone is not, and nothing is live yet

Measured on the build at 85deaf42 (v1165 plus the render pass), lab figures from Chromium on the laptop at three device profiles, three runs each, with the live round trips and 164 approved rows read from production. No road pushes data to an open screen today: every change waits for a poll, a return to the app, or a reload.

No live channel existsNo WebSocket or server-sent stream in any of the three. Web Push only wakes a screen, carrying no data, and the screen then reads. The Counter's order book is already a Durable Object, but it only answers requests and runs alarms; it pushes nothing to a page. The Desk's ledger is baked into the page at build, so any ledger change is a new build and a full reload.

Where the time goes

  1. The Desk builds everything on open. 2.8 s of blocked main thread on a phone, before the first tap is answered.
  2. The Desk redraws a whole place per tap. 300 ms to 1 s on a phone; Record, Network, Pricing, Today and Analysis are the heaviest.
  3. Data rides the build. A ledger change is fold, build, deploy and reload: a minute at best.
  4. Polls, not pushes. 2, 10, 30 and 60 s polls, and three roads with none at all.
  5. QR draws each code on the tap. About 250 ms on a phone.

Method

  • Laptop 1440 by 900, no throttling. Tablet 820 by 1180, touch, CPU 2 times slower, 30 Mbps. Phone 390 by 844, touch, CPU 4 times slower, 9 Mbps down, 60 ms (DevTools' Fast 4G).
  • Served from this checkout on 127.0.0.1, brotli as Cloudflare sends it. The Counter draws the heaviest real account (CS6-BS) under a fixture key, as the counter-render skill does. Nothing signed in to a live site.
  • Lab estimates: throttling scales this laptop's CPU, not his phone's. Between two full runs the laptop Desk moved by up to 40%, so compare medians of three, and read a change under 20% as noise.
  • The approve-to-live figures are read from production's draft table (read only). The live round trips are curl from the laptop.

Re-run after any change and compare against the filed figures:

node tools/perf-baseline.mjs --label after --runs 3 --qr ..\qr-command

The filed figures are docs/perf/baseline-2026-10-19.json; each run writes its own to test/_perf/<label>/results.json.
