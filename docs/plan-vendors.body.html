Vendors beside Customers, and Orders for both sides

Salt Admin gets a Vendors place built as the mirror of Customers: a list, a page for each vendor with its prices, deliveries and notes, and Log a purchase as the one way to record a purchase, the twin of Order for them. Orders holds sales and purchases in the same four stages, on one order card. Record goes, and each of its forms moves to the page it acts on. Pricing does not move.

Log a purchase the twin of Order for them

  • Opened from the vendor page's Orders group and foot, and from each vendor row's pill: a sheet with the vendor filled in and fixed. A vendor not on the list yet is added first with New vendor; a purchase may name a vendor whose Add ID waits under Approve, as today.
  • Its fields are Record's purchase fields in the same order: Book, Units, Price, Freight, Paid now (RM), Goods received now, Handover, Ordered on; and one new, Expected (the day you expect it, optional), shown only while the lot is not wholly received, so a landed lot is never given a day ahead of it. Add a book stays and carries Expected too.
  • Its reading is today's: the cost a unit against the book's cost now (/all/maker/trade-reading). A read; it never holds Send.
  • Send posts /all/maker/inputs/purchase with the body Record's form sends today, key for key, plus expectedOn. It is kept on the phone until the Desk confirms it, under the same storage key, so an entry kept today still sends from here. It waits under Approve.
  • Nothing paid and nothing received books a purchase order (pending). Paid only books it on the way. Goods received books it landed. Rate the delivery shows on its card once the lot is booked (a lot has no rid before Approve).

  • Filters All, Sales and Purchases, each counted, under the stage tabs; the find field takes any code on either side; in All each row carries a Sale or Purchase chip.
  • One order card, with a side. A purchase is drawn by the sale card's own function: the vendor's code, the book's mark, units and total, the stage words above, and the same three controls. One stage pill: Goods received while goods are due, then Money paid once the goods are in and money is owed. One Change row opening the one Change sheet: Paid now (RM), Goods received, units, price, freight and Expected, each a field, as a sale's Change sheet holds a sale's. Cancel the order and Never delivered last, as danger rows. Rate the delivery is asked inside Goods received (on time, quality, units short, a line); a later mark, like Late's note, is a row in the card's log, so the controls stay three.
  • To pay and Money paid's figure are the engine's own oweRM (engine/position.mjs 1237): the total less what was paid, freight outside it, which is what the lot's guard measures. Verified On the book, 19 of the 26 lots with freight were paid their total and 6 their total plus freight, so the freight is said beside the figure, never folded into it.
  • Late has no act of its own: Add a note with a follow-up day, a row in the card's log, the day kept in the note's next.
  • Needs you gains the purchase cards of To do, in the Action card's one shape.
  • Sales still come from the order book and purchases from the Book. The page draws them together and stores nothing together: orders and the book stay two objects (your answer of 25 Oct 2026).
  • Approve, Payments they sent and Ledger check are unchanged.
