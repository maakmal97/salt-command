Your answer H, built with the band

A discount moves only when you count or buy

Verified: the built engine on the book of 11 Nov 2026, against the live one's.
  1. A discount is a place between the Median and the Floor, and the Floor carries the unpaid share. Measured live, that share ticked with every handover, so a discount could shift by RM 5 now and then.
  2. The share is now re-measured only at a count, a recorded loss or a landed lot. Selling, gifts and write-offs between those days move no Floor and no discount; they are taken in at the next one.
  3. Today it reads 21.1%, as measured at the count of 10 Nov 2026. The live Desk, still ticking, reads 20.9%.
  4. So 2 of 13 Floors read RM 5 higher at the switch than the live Desk quotes today: 50 units RM 3,025 to RM 3,030, 100 units RM 6,050 to RM 6,055. That is the partner's price at those lots, which nobody has bought. Every other Floor is the same.
  5. This list is checked once more at the push. If any price at 1 unit or at a usual lot differs from what is shown here, nothing ships and you are asked again.

How it lands
