The rule

One stream, from 03 Nov 2026

  1. Every order is a site order on the buyer's account. The customer places it on Salt Counter, or he places it as them with Order for them on Salt Admin. Accept rows it.
  2. One row a book, keyed by the order. A sheet of several books with one delivery is one trip, its delivery on the first line.
  3. An associate's friend books to their -R account. The friend is named as their customer and credited nothing.
  4. A gift or reward unit rides on an order. The fold raises it and hands it over with that order or the next.
  5. Every later move is a stage of that order. Payment, handover, amendment and cancellation each go through the order, never a fresh row.
  6. Nothing booked is rewritten. A change is a Correction with its own date; the row as booked stays in its trail.
