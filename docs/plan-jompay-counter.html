Verified in code, 06 Nov 2026
Already private

The Counter's Worker reads Salt QR's live suspensions through a service binding, Worker to Worker. The customer's phone never contacts Salt QR for them.

Everything fits

Salt QR's bundle carries every payload, account number, biller code and Ref-1. All 14 codes, 122 to 216 bytes, fit the Counter's own QR encoder, whose largest version holds 362.

Links out today

Salt QR's address is baked into the Counter's page, and Scan a code and Open the payment page open it in a new tab.

Cannot hide

A DuitNow code carries the account holder's name, and the payer's bank app shows it. That is DuitNow itself, not Salt QR.
