JomPAY on Salt Counter

A customer chooses JomPAY on the pay sheet, beside Transfer and Scan a code, and is handed to Salt QR's JomPAY view at the right biller code, Ref-1 and amount. Their username never sits where a JomPAY form reads it as Ref-1. Plan only: nothing is built until your answers.

Verdict

Most of the road is already there

Verified in code, 05 Nov 2026
Already there

Salt QR has the JomPAY view, the order book accepts JomPAY as a method, and Salt Admin already names it.

Missing

The Counter's link refuses the JomPAY rail, and its pay sheet has no JomPAY option.

Suspended today

Instapay, the one JomPAY account, is suspended in Salt QR, so JomPAY shows only once you lift it in Salt QR's admin.

The risk

Ref-1 names your wallet

On a JomPAY form, Ref-1 is the Instapay id the money is paid into, a fixed 13-digit number. If the sheet told a customer to put their username "in the reference", they would type it as Ref-1. The bank refuses letters there, so no money goes astray, but the payment fails while their claim is filed. Every JomPAY word on the sheet says Ref-1 comes from Salt QR, and the username is never shown as a reference on JomPAY.

Basis

What the code says

  1. Salt QR. One account runs JomPAY, instapay: biller 11445 and its Ref-1, with Ref-2 shown as a dash by your rule of 14 Sep 2026. Its link reader already takes the JomPAY rail.
  2. The Counter's link. payHref (tools/paysync.mjs) writes only the QR and Transfer rails. stmt/pay.js already marks which accounts run JomPAY.
  3. The pay sheet. It offers Transfer, Scan a code and cash (stmt/page.js). Its accounts list follows the link, so JomPAY needs only the link and an option.
  4. The order book. pickRail already takes JomPAY with an account that runs it. Salt Admin shows the method as "JomPAY to Instapay".
  5. Live suspensions. The sheet lays Salt QR's live suspensions over its list each time it opens. The practice accounts get the same live list, so today their sheet would hide JomPAY too.

Said

Not in this version

  1. A claim naming a suspended account is accepted. The order book checks against the suspensions baked in at deploy, not the live ones, on every rail. This is older than JomPAY. The fix is its own version: read the live flags once in the Worker and pass them into the order book. If that read fails, the baked flags stand.
  2. Credit cards. JomPAY takes a card only at banks that offer it, and only if the biller accepts cards. Whether biller 11445 does is unverified, so no word on the sheet promises one.
