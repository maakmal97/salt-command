One head for every place: the title, the parts, the status, then the headline

Every place on Salt Admin, Salt Counter and later Salt Desk opens the same way on every screen: its title with no gap above it, then one row of sliding parts, then one row of status, both rows the same height on every place, then the place's headline. Filters leave the navigation and become a menu at the head of the list they filter. Display only: nothing that prices, books, folds, routes or holds money moves.

salt-command at 29c6443a (v1562 live), Ambient 0.7.31. Built only on your answers to the four asks at the end.

Each check proved red by its own mutation before its green counts.
  • The older checks that read the old head are re-aimed with a dated note, never deleted: the phone's hero heights, the bar's status (test/work-bar.mjs), the +'s hiding on Record, Needs you's kinds and the Accounts filters as segments.
  • Drawn and measured with admin-snap and counter-render, with real data and with long words, every screenshot looked at; the 390 and 1180 shots sent to you after it lands.
  • The skeleton audit of v1546 run again on all 21 places and parts: identical per size.
