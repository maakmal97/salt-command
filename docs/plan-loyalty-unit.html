Loyalty unit · build plan · 20 Oct 2026

A customer's loyalty unit counts units at their price, and rides the associate reward's road

One Desk version, after the associate reward is live: the engine counts a customer's salt units bought at or above their own price, one unit earned at every 20; the fold books it as its own line at cost on the same delivery, as the associate reward does; a late payment or a write-off pauses it; the customer sees a % only; Salt Admin's This customer card shows the meter, their cadence and the ringgit, and pairs it with the % already suggested, which stays his tap.

1 · The one thing

His rule, and the road it rides

His rule of 20 Oct 2026 (canvas thread b1deced4, board V55)Salt only. A customer earns 1 unit at cost for every 20 units they buy at or above their price (the Set Price lifted by their %). The unit joins a paid order as its own line at cost, on the same delivery. It is paired with the % the This customer card already suggests (suggestPct) for a customer who reorders on cadence and pays on time; the % stays his tap. A late payment or a write-off pauses it. The customer sees their progress only as a %, never RM.
  • One road, not two. The associate reward plan (docs/plan-assoc-reward.html, branch claude/friendly-babbage-8f0fe3, built by session assoc-reward-build) already designs every moving part this needs: a walk beside rewardAccount (engine/position.mjs 46 to 76), the fold-raised reward row and its delivery with the order (engine/apply.mjs bookItem, beside 881 to 893), the drafter's listRM stamp of their own price (cardAt, src/orders.js 293 to 307), the Accept verdict and the rwd word ("cross", "given") through CUSTOMER_FIELDS (stmt/orders.js 288). This plan adds a second walk and a loyal mark on the same row; nothing else is new but Admin's reading and the customer's %.
  • What differs from the associate's: it counts units, not margin, so no cost moves the step (no rwdStep, no openings at RM); only rows at or above their price count; it pauses.
  • Not a price. The loyal row is a gift (goodwill, total: 0, as giftEntry makes one), which W.pricedSales (position.mjs 697), pbPriced (engine/snapshot.mjs 198) and recentRates (src/drafter.js 451) already drop. The % he may raise is his tap through the existing Their % road (makerTierSet, src/maker-inputs.js 714 to 733), proposed for Approve as every % is.
  • Waits on: the Salt Admin A to C release, then the associate reward version on master (its P1, P3, P4 and P5 are this plan's footing). Nothing is built here until both are live and he says yes.

3 · Data path

From a paid order to a unit on the van

  1. The walk (engine, new loyaltyWalk(book, code, D) beside the associate plan's rewardWalk). From REWARD.stated.salt.loyal.since (new, {every: 20, since}), over the party's own salt SELL rows, in pOn then rid order. A row counts its final qty when: (a) paid in full by any road (txDates(s).fullPaid, position.mjs 470 to 488) on pOn; (b) at or above their price per unit: txGoods(s)/qty ≥ listRM/listQty, the drafter stamping listQty beside listRM, and an amendment (restateOrder) re-stamping both from the card for the new size, so a short close or a re-size reads true; a row with no listRM counts nothing; (c) not itself late: no delay for it in payDelays(sales, party, pOn, rules), called with today = pOn for each row (position.mjs 344 to 374), so a delay open on that day counts even if settled later; (d) the party not paused on pOn: lateOffer over those delays says late && run < lateResetRepeat (the V5b2c rule, lateHistory, engine/stages.mjs 34 to 39), a late on the same day pausing (lateOffer sorts prompt after late, 388) and the row's own prompt payment counting in run; or any row with txWrittenOff(s) > 0 (303) dated on or before pOn, which pauses for good (Ask 5). A pause keeps progress and never resets it. A gift, a redemption, a reward or loyal row and a written-off row count nothing. Every 20 units crosses once; one big order may cross twice. Returns {earned, units, every, next, paused, why, crossings}.
  2. Who walks: a party rewardAccount does not class as an associate (53 to 56), not in partners, not a supplier, not a -R bucket (Ask 1). A customer later made an associate stops walking that day; loyal rows already raised stand. No opening: everyone starts at 0 units on since, since older rows carry no listRM (Ask 7).
  3. The unit (engine/apply.mjs bookItem, the associate plan's fold-raised row). Shaped as a gift: {customer, product: "salt", qty: 1, total: 0, cost, cash: 0, goodwill: true, deliveredQty: 0, date, rewardOf: rid, auto: true, loyal: true}, read by txStat as the existing gift is (position.mjs 449 to 456) and joining W.goodwillRM (698), an expense, as a gift does; no rebate, no rebateKg, cost the crossing day's latestCogs. Both belts: rewardAccount, inputResource and rewardReservation also skip loyal rows, so the unit is never read as a redemption against a customer's reward. due = walk earned less the party's rows with rewardOf && loyal (cancelled included); the associate plan's own due becomes rewardOf && !loyal. The same delivery road (the handover that fills the rewardOf row delivers it and hands a −1 move to applyEntry before the roll), the same "rides the next salt handover" if paid after the goods went out, the same STALE flag on an undone payment, never withdrawn.
  4. The verdict at Accept (the associate plan's rewardCrossOf, engine/stages.mjs, gains a loyal branch): this order's qty counts if its agreed total per unit is at or above the card's for that size and the party is not paused today; it crosses if paid units plus this qty reach the next 20. Accept stamps rwd = "cross" on the entry and rwdLoyal: true on the Pending row, hashed into stageDigest and offerDigest only when set, named in both mark whitelists (stmt/orders.js 881 to 898, 1224 to 1242) and the leak scans. openSnapshot says "cross" for that key while rwdLoyal stands unpaid, re-reading the walk's paused and the at-or-above test; the return leg clears it by an explicit null when a pause began or another order crossed first, and "given" is terminal, as in the associate plan.
  5. The % (tools/book.mjs): the publish seals, for every non-associate, products[].loyal = {next} on salt only, a share of one at two decimals, beside the associate's card (405 to 430). No units, no step, no RM, no paused word. At two decimals it exposes their units to go, which they know from their own orders; no cost travels.
  6. His reading: GET /all/maker/customer-reading?code=&product=salt, four edits (stmt/maker.js internalOperation 320 to 367 and the deskCall allowlist 76 to 103; src/worker.js a route beside 734 to 745 and the internal allowlist 552), handler makerCustomerReading in src/maker-inputs.js. It returns their last six paid salt rates (recentRates, n 6), a unit now (their card at their median size), the highest, their cadence (custCadence moved from master 17895 to 17901 into the engine as cadenceOf, the median gap between order dates, null under two; a customer has no -R, so pricedSales' merge is moot), next due (last order plus cadence, less today), the walk's units, step, paused and why, cost (latestCogs today), the suggestion byte for byte as suggestOf gives it, and the two halves of the gain. A GET, read behind both locks; it writes nothing.
  7. The gain, in two halves, at their median size scaled to 20 units: the % on his tap is 20 × (rate at suggested % − rate at their %); the unit is its cost. Shown as "+10%: RM 23.20 more per 20 units · the loyalty unit RM 7.20 · net RM 16.00". Hidden when the suggestion is at or below their held %, and the pairing line shows only when not paused and the snapshot profile's loyal flag holds (engine/snapshot.mjs 213); otherwise the suggestion stands alone, as today.
  8. Fixture. Every test account is an associate today (tools/testfix.mjs 144, 367). 0000-0002 becomes a plain customer (assoc: false), with loyal.next 0.7 and one order that crosses; the friend-order and card features stay on 0000-0000 and 0000-0001 (Ask 8). The assoc: true asserts across test/*.mjs and testfix.mjs 452, 467 and 551 are re-aimed.

7 · Not in this version

Left out, and why

  • A paused word to the customer: they see the % only; the pause shows as a % that stops rising. His reminder of a late payment already exists in the chase.
  • Automatic %: never. The suggestion and its pairing are words on his card; Use it is his tap and goes to Approve.
  • Counting history before the release: older rows carry no stamp of their price.
