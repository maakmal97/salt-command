1 · The rule

Who says what

One event, one surfaceAn event that waits on your decision belongs to the app where you decide it: Salt Admin. An event that only reports belongs to Salt Desk. A customer's own news goes to their Salt Counter. No event is pushed twice.
  • Salt Admin acts. New orders, customer lines, payment claims, cancellations, their yes or no to an offer, rows to approve. A tap opens the card where you answer.
  • Salt Desk informs. A version live, a fold refused, a suite red, a refund you hold, a stock light turning yellow or red, the morning digest. A tap opens the Desk page that shows it.
  • Salt Counter is unchanged. Each customer's order and pay news, the chase, the thread and the bulletin, sealed to their phone as today.

3 · Mechanism

How Salt Admin gets its own banners

  1. Its own service worker. /all/sw.js, served behind both locks with Service-Worker-Allowed: /all and registered at scope /all, so it controls Admin pages and the customer's root worker never sees them.
  2. Its own subscriptions. POST /all/push/subscribe and unsubscribe, filed at Counter KV opush:<hash>, outside push:, so no customer count or Sign out everywhere ever reads them. The Counter's own VAPID pair, as the origin requires.
  3. A bell on Admin. The same ask as the customer's bell, asked only from a tap, on every view until on.
  4. Sent where the event happens. The Counter wakes Admin at the moment a customer places, writes, claims, cancels or answers an offer, sealed {k, o} like a customer's wake, words by kind, never an amount or a name. The Desk's drafter asks the Counter to wake Admin over the existing DESK_MAKER binding for rows to approve.
  5. The Desk's banners rewritten. public/sw.js banners() keeps only the inform list. The order wake in reconcileOrders and pushIfDrafted stop ringing the Desk.
  6. No gap at the switch. The Desk keeps the order banners until Admin reports at least one subscribed device, then drops them by itself.
  • Test accounts wake nobody. A move by 0000-0000 to 0000-0002 lands in its fixture and sends no Admin banner; /all/test likewise.
  • Rule 2 holds. A line's text is the customer's own, already shown today; no roster code, amount or place reaches a title.
