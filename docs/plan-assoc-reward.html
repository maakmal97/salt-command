Associate reward · build plan · 20 Oct 2026

The associate reward steps with landed cost, and arrives with the order

One Desk version: the engine counts an associate's salt margin against 10 units at today's landed cost, the fold books the reward unit by itself once a crossing order is paid and hands it over with that delivery, Salt Counter says so with no figure, and Salt Admin shows the ringgit. A customer who is not an associate counts against 1.5 times that step and redeems nothing: their reward is his basis for a discount he gives under their Set Price, shown on Salt Admin. Nothing prices: the Floor, the Set Price and every customer's % read nothing new.

1 · The one thing

His five rules, and what already exists

His rules of 20 Oct 2026Salt only. One unit per 10 units' landed cost of margin on their purchase and resale (the step is 10 × latestCogs: RM 580 at RM 58 today). A received order that takes them over is told, with no figure, to pay now; once paid it gains 1 reward unit, its own line, at cost, on the same delivery. The ringgit is his information only, net of the discounts he gave below their Set Price since the last reward. The associate sees a % only.
  • His answers of 20 Oct 2026: Asks 1 to 6, 8 and 9 as recommended. Ask 7 his own: a customer who is not an associate earns a unit at 1.5 times an associate's step (15 units' landed cost, RM 870 today); they cannot redeem, and he uses it as a basis to give a discount on their orders under their own Set Price.
  • Built on, not beside: the reward already runs end to end. REWARD.stated.salt (master 18270 to 18288: RM 500 a customer, RM 470 an associate, R3 at 1/1.2) reaches the mirror as state key REWARD (tools/book.mjs 247 to 254, 511) and the fold as ctx.reward (tools/fold.mjs 72 to 78, 213). rewardAccount (engine/position.mjs 46) gives earned, applied and held; a redemption is a sales row with goodwill and rebate (src/drafter.js 1436 to 1467); offsets and cover spend the same balance (src/maker-inputs.js 653, 695, 232).
  • Rule 5 already holds on the Counter: the card's reward is five keys, next a share of one unit (tools/book.mjs 357, 457); the suite scans the card for margin, cost and floor words (test/verify.mjs 21739, 25272). The cover line carries no amount (salt-open-decisions, 27 Sep 2026). This plan adds one word to an order and no figure anywhere a customer reads.
  • Not a price: the reward row is goodwill and rebate, which W.pricedSales (position.mjs 697) and pbPriced (engine/snapshot.mjs 198) already drop, and latestCogs reads purchases only. 3 · Data path

From a paid order to a unit on the van

  1. The walk (engine, new rewardWalk(book, id) beside rewardAccount). From REWARD.stated.salt.associate.since, over the associate's own code and their -R account (ownerCode), salt only: each priced row counts when paid in full by any road (the engine's txDates(s).fullPaid, position.mjs 470 to 488, never paidOn alone, which apply.mjs 368 sets only for cash and never for a part settled in kind), in the order of its pOn then rid (rids are monotonic, so a shared day is deterministic), its margin the row's goods less units at the row's own cost, as rewardMargin counts it. A gift (goodwill, not a reward) has no total, so it is never fullPaid: it counts on its delivery day (dOn), its cost off. A redemption is not an order; a written-off row never pays. Progress starts at the opening share times the step on since. Each time progress reaches the step, measured at that day's latestCogs (position.mjs 639, which takes onOrBefore), one unit is earned and the step comes off. A row stamped with rwdStep at Accept (item 8) is measured at the lesser of that step and the day's, so a lot landing between Accept and payment never breaks the promise. If COGS falls with no row paid, progress can stand over today's step without a crossing: next holds at 1 and the unit is earned by the next paid row, which is the one it rides on. The step is 10 × the rate, from REWARD.stated.salt.associate.times (10). It returns earned, the progress in RM, the step today, next (progress over today's step, 0 to 1), each crossing (its rid and step) and the discounts given since the last crossing.
  2. One balance: rewardAccount reads the walk for an associate when associate.times is set, so redemption, offsets, cover, the reservation check (entryReservationFault) and the card all move with it. A customer walks the same way at REWARD.stated.salt.customer.times (15, his 1.5 times), over their own code (a customer has no -R), with their own opening; the RM 500 stated figure retires. The master's networkStats (18400 to 18410) takes unit and toNext from the walk, so the card's next (tools/book.mjs 457) needs no change. The master's inlined copy follows by tools/engine.mjs --sync.
  3. A customer redeems nothing (his answer to Ask 7). rewardAccount returns redeemable: false for a non-associate, and every spend road reads it: the redeem input (src/maker-inputs.js 1054 to 1076), the cover plan (makerCoverPlan 232 to 248, which was customers' alone, so it retires; the master's coverPlan 18643 with it), entryReservationFault and the drafter's redeem and cover modes refuse it, and no fold raises a unit for them. Every road is refused from one place: inputResource (position.mjs 139 to 152) returns an error when !redeemable, and entryReservationFault (119 to 128, which today calls rewardAccount and reads only its error) reads inputResource instead, so input admission (src/input-admission.js 85 to 110), the reservation check and a Correction setting rebate, rebateKg or coverUnits on a customer's row (rewardReservation 89 to 103, drafter.js 210) all refuse alike. Salt Admin's Record › Rewards catalogue (makerInputCatalog, src/maker-inputs.js 566 to 573; stmt/owner.js 2372 to 2386 and 556) gives a customer action: null and the basis, and says "basis", never "free". The fold has no reservation check (tools/fold.mjs 46 to 50), so a customer redeem drafted before the switch is caught by the version's queue check (P10), which drains every pending and approved draft first. What they earn is his basis, a running bank: their opening (held units, earned less applied, at latestCogs on since, Ask 10), plus each unit earned since at its crossing day's cost, less every discount given under their Set Price since the switch (listRM less the total, item 7). A discount over the bank carries forward against later units; Admin shows nought, never a minus. A discount also lowers that order's margin and so slows the next unit: the same double effect his rule 4 gives an associate, intended and asserted. Information only: he gives a discount by the price he sets at Accept, as today, never under the Floor; nothing reads the basis to price. Old rows keep "Part paid from your reward" (dated records); no new order gets it.
  4. A customer who becomes an associate (an appointment, a sale row with their ref, an introduction or R0_NETWORK all flip rewardAccount, 53 to 56): the walk chooses its step by the opening's shape, so a {held} opening keeps the customer walk until an {earned} one exists, and the fold writes that associate opening (the same % carried, earned as the associate walk then reads it) on any entry that flips the flag, not only an appointment. No lump is raised on their next payment. Their basis up to that day stays his information.
  5. Openings in ledger/book.json as REWARD_OPEN, {code: {earned, share}}, for every associate ({earned, share}, the old earned) and every customer with margin on the book ({held, share}: held, never earned, since a customer has no reward row to net their October covers s243, s244 and s261), written once by a tool from the desk's own networkStats and customerRewards at the switch through writeBookFile, then booksync --sync. Book facts sit in the book; the rule (since, times) sits in REWARD, which already rides to the mirror and the fold.
  6. The fold raises the unit (engine/apply.mjs bookItem, beside refundOnCancel and refundOnOverpay at 881 to 893). After any move on a salt SELL row of an associate: due = walk earned − opening earned − auto reward rows already on the book for them. For each due unit it appends a sales row: {customer: code, product: "salt", qty: 1, total: cost, cost, cash: 0, settledRM: cost, rebate: true, rebateKg: 1, goodwill: true, deliveredQty: 0, date, rewardOf: rid, auto: true, given}, cost the crossing day's latestCogs rate (Ask 9), its rid from nextRid, its note written by the engine as a refund's is. It runs once per entry for the party the entry touched, on both branches of bookItem: an amendment and a new row drafted paid in full (drafter.js 1674, apply.mjs 914 to 926), so a cash walk-in that crosses earns at once. The rows counted are every row with a rewardOf for that party, cancelled ones included, so a re-run adds nothing and a row he cancels by hand is not raised again. rewardOf is the row that crossed when it was booked and is never recomputed; due is a count. No draft, so no D1 CHECK change: the row bypasses the draft table as a refund does, and sales is in migrations/0010's list anyway.
  7. On the same delivery. When a handover Correction takes the rewardOf row to its full quantity (apply.mjs 615 to 632), the fold sets the reward row's deliveredQty to 1, its deliveredOn and handover to the order's, and returns a kg move of −1. The roll is fixed by planBatch before apply (apply.mjs 989 to 1000, 1016) and bookItem's own moves are only words, so bookItem hands its kg moves back through c and applyEntry adds them to entryMoves before the roll; applyInto's ROLLED sentence follows. Without this the shelf would read one unit over per reward (position.mjs 711 to 721). Approve's preview, made before apply, cannot foresee it, so its does lines read one unit short on that batch; the ROLLED sentence is right. A reward raised after its order went out in full rides on that associate's next salt handover (Ask 4). The batch books in event order, so cash and handover in one batch deliver it together.
  8. Undone, flagged, never withdrawn. If a payment is undone and the walk no longer earns an uncancelled booked unit, the fold's moves say STALE reward on that rid, as refundStale does; he removes the row in ledger/book.json, and the words say remove, never cancel: a cancelled reward row still counts, so one cancelled and then earned again by a re-made payment would never be raised. A departed associate earns into the walk and gets no row until they return (rewardAccount's departed rule).
  9. Their price, for the discount (rule 4). No row records it today (the Desk mapper found none; asked reaches a note only, stages.mjs 166). From since, the drafter stamps listRM on every salt SELL row, an associate's and a customer's: their own price for that size from the PRICING cards (cardAt, the figure previewAck already returns, src/orders.js 346), so it reads and never prices. Given = the sum of listRM − total where positive, over rows counted since the last crossing. A partner's card is the Floor, so theirs is always nought. Net = cost less given, never under nought. Information only (Ask 6).
  10. The verdict at Accept (the prepay road, four points, src/orders.js 337, drafter.js 1906 and 1923, orders.js 415). previewAck adds rewardCrossOf (engine, stages.mjs beside advanceOf): this order's margin at its row cost on top of the walk's paid progress alone; it crosses if that reaches today's step. Other unpaid orders are not counted ahead of it, so paying it always crosses unless another order of theirs is paid first and crosses, in which case they already have the unit. Stamped entry.rwd = "cross" and, on the Pending row, rwdStep (the step at Accept), hashed into stageDigest and offerDigest only when set (so every other hash is unchanged), carried by ackOnApproval into moveOrder, kept by decideDesk and applyEvent (stmt/orders.js 1112 to 1116, 1144) and the practice road (stmt/maker.js EXAMPLE_FIELDS 246 to 281). The preview also returns his reading (P5).
  11. Kept true. reconcileOrders' work loop visits only orders with something untold (ordersOwing, stmt/orders.js 372 to 387, 516 to 520), which an unpaid acknowledged order and a paid, handed-over one both lack. So rwd rides the v764 return leg instead, which carries what the book holds per ledgerKey to every open row (stmt/orders.js 908). Its desk side is the mirror's OPEN.byKey, built by openSnapshot (tools/book.mjs 198 to 226, 509) and read at src/orders.js 1586: openSnapshot puts rwd on each key's entry, "given" when a row's rewardOf is that key's rid, "cross" while its own rwdStep stands unpaid. The leg then carries it: "given" once a reward row's rewardOf is the order's ledger row, and terminal, as paid only rises, so a lagging mirror never flips it back; "cross" cleared by an explicit null only after a payment of the associate's own on another order earned the unit, never on a lot (the promise holds through rwdStep, and a flip on a lot would tell them COGS rose). An undone payment on another order lowers progress, which rwdStep does not cover, so an undo of theirs clears cross too. Both whitelists take it (stmt/orders.js 881 to 898 and 1224 to 1242).
  12. What travels. rwd joins CUSTOMER_FIELDS (stmt/orders.js 288) as one word, "cross" or "given". No figure, margin or cost reaches customerView, the card or the live document. The card keeps its five reward keys. Admin's ringgit reads come through Access only. rwdStep is ten times the cost on a ledger row: it stays out of OPEN.byKey (which carries the word only) and out of every statement line (tools/make_statements.mjs 889 to 913 picks its fields one by one; listRM is the drafter's stamp alone and never joins the input fields at position.mjs 1464), and the leak scans name it.

7 · Not in this version

Left out, and why

  • A % on each order: a % before and after one order lets them work out the landed cost (Ask 8). The card's % stays. The crossing word itself carries one bit an order: with the card's next it bounds the cost at total / (qty + 10(1 − next)), at whole-% precision; accepted as low bandwidth, as the card's bar already is.
  • Held units from before the switch (CS6-PER 1, CE4-AD 1, CM4-TO 1, CJ4-OKR 3 while departed): still taken by the existing redemption, offset and cover roads; nothing automatic spends them.
  • The This customer card (canvas V38): held on his profit-rule answer.
