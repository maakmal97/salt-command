3 · Design

How each part works

Order as them

  • The account card's Order for them opens their page by the master with an act flag, owner only. Prices, the basket and the order sheet go live; Pay, claims, Cancel, the thread, devices and passkeys stay read only.
  • Each line Places through /all/maker/customers/<u>/orders, behind both locks and the owner email, into the same placeOrder. No customer session is made.
  • The route takes what a customer's Place takes (sheet, window, sealed spot, Order again, wanted by) and refuses a note, a group order or a Negotiate ask. His words reach them on the order's thread, word-checked.
  • Their sealed list is the price; the digest, the grid, five open and the hours hold. Their hold does not stop him.
  • "Placed for you" wakes them and stands on their card; his card says "You placed".

His Place is his Accept

  • The same request previews and accepts at the total he saw, as a group order's close does today. The row drafts and approves with no second tap.
  • If the accept fails, the order stays Placed and his card says why. A Place at 23:59 accepted at 00:00 waits under Approve, said on the card.

The stall sale, on the stream

  • "Handed over and paid now", owner only: the request accepts, hands over (closed at the units) and takes the cash through today's roads. No new entry kind. Accept sends no pay-now banner; the payment's own wake tells them.
  • A past date only with that ticked: one date on the row, the handover (the Counter's handover event too) and the payment, so nothing reads late. Not before the book's newest purchase or 21 Oct 2026, not in the future, within 30 days.
  • Two equal sales to one customer on one day stay two rows (second).
  • Lines of one sheet with one delivery are one trip, accepted in one request on one day.
  • "For a friend" names their end buyer by code. Under the Floor stays refused.

Everyone has an account

  • Walk-in, ready. At the stall he adds the customer (name and locality, his passphrase); the next run binds a ready account and Show a code signs their phone in. The pool stays at ten or more; Needs you warns under three.
  • Join by link. An associate asks for a link for one person; he approves it; it works once. Shut, declined or spent, it shows the one shut page.
  • The referee's page. Name or nickname (Johan, John, Jonah) and locality, both required. The page seals them to his key before they leave the phone: no name reaches the cloud in the clear.
  • Back to him. It cannot be minted on their phone, since the code comes from the name and the name lives only in his vault. Needs you shows the new customer; his one unlock opens it, derives the code, files the vault and adds the ID with the referrer.
  • Signed in by itself. Once the account opens, a hand-over key goes to the page that sent the form, and to no other; the phone is remembered and offered a passkey. The username is the pool's.
  • R3. Their orders in the 7 days after the account opens count toward the referrer's reward step, as the associate's own buying does, once paid in full. Only an associate can refer.

One list on the Counter

  • Orders lists every order: their site orders, then book lines no order holds, as Earlier by month.
  • The page joins a statement line to an order by day (the row's or the acknowledgement's), goods (or the total before a close), delivery and book. No key leaves the Desk.
  • Account holds the money: balance, paid, delivery, refunds, owed. Full statement opens the whole document in a sheet.

Ordering from Order

  • Prices becomes Order, second after Home. New order and Home's start tiles land there.
  • A stepper per product walks that book's own sealed sizes, the price live, Add to the basket. A grid book takes a typed size.
  • The sheet keeps only the way, the place and the check.
  • The marks become coins: the book's hue as a ring, the shape inside, the letter under it; the restock light a dot and its word. Drawn on a canvas first.
