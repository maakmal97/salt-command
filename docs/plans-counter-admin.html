1. Needs you that tells the truth

Prompted 15 Oct 2026: the page read "Nothing needs you. Not read: orders and rows." One malformed queue key (mine, from the laptop) blanked the whole read; fixed the same evening.

What changes

  1. A failed read leads the page: "Couldn't read orders and rows. Retry", in place of the all-clear. "Nothing needs you" shows only when every read succeeded.
  2. One bad entry never blanks the rest. The Desk's queue review skips a malformed device key, names it, and returns everything else. Today one key throws for all.
  3. Money owed as Soon cards: each customer past term, oldest first, the amount and a Record payment button. On 15 Oct that would have shown CN3-WM MYR 255 (over the ceiling), CJ4-BJ MYR 100 and CS6-PER.
  4. Goods owed as Soon cards: paid but not handed over (CM4-TO 5.5 units, CE4-CHE 1.5), with Handover.
  5. The Desk card names its items, or goes: "The Desk shows 4" says nothing you can act on.

Guard

  • Cards read figures the Desk already serves (txAdvance, the open orders); nothing new is computed on the Counter.
  • The suite drives a failed read and a malformed key and proves both red.

3. Orders across several books

Prompted 15 Oct 2026: CS6-PER's salt, oil and rice of 14 Oct (s264 to s266) were three cards, each handed over and paid on its own; the rows carry no trip.

Today

  • The book ties a buyer's rows of one day by trip: one delivery on one row, one tap approves the trip. Counter Admin's trade form makes them.
  • The order card, Handover and cash are per row; nothing shows the rows belong together. A customer on the site orders one book at a time.

What you would see

  1. One card for a trip: owed, paid and handed across every line, then one line per book (mark, units, total, handed).
  2. Handover all, with a per-line field for a part handover; one handover per row, approved as one card (plan 2).
  3. One payment split across the lines oldest first by the engine's claimAlloc, shown before it is sent.
  4. "These are one order" on a buyer's same-day rows with no trip: sets the trip, a Correction each.
  5. On the customer's site, later: a basket of several books placed as one order, arriving as one trip.

Guard

  • Each row's share passes rowRoom and the fold's payFault; one refused share refuses the batch.
  • Salt keeps the drift, the other books their fixed boards; nothing reprices a line. One delivery stays on one row (tripFault).
  • A book short of stock holds only its own line.

4. Easy revert

Prompted 15 Oct 2026: s267's RM220 cash booked by mistake. Today an undo is Record, Correction, pick the row, pick each field, type its old value. Reviewed by Fable the same day.

What you would see

  • On every order card a History: each booked move, newest first ("RM220 cash, 15 Oct", "2 units collected, 15 Oct").
  • Undo on the newest move only, of any kind, so undos peel back in order. It opens Now and After and one button, and lands under Approve as one card.
  • Cash and bank moves only; a payment in kind (offset, reward) stays a hand fold.

What each undo sets

  • A payment: cash less that payment; paidOn cleared if what remains is below what is owed.
  • A handover: deliveredQty less those units; deliveredOn and handover cleared at zero.
  • A restatement: the figures it replaced, from its own record on the row.

Guard, from Fable's review

  1. Money on its way. The undo sets an absolute figure, and today's checks only measure money going up, so no undo while anything is on its way to the row, as Cancellation already does.
  2. A site order's page. Reuse the order book's existing fix event (unpay, unmove) and remove the payment line, or the next reconcile books it again.
  3. Refunds are never closed by an undo; one it would strand is said on the sheet.
  4. Restatements on a site order go through restateOrder, never a plain Correction, or the order loses its row.
  5. Late-payer drift reads the row's trail; the undo marks its step undone so the drift ignores it.
  6. Stock: an undone handover after a count is flagged. Price: never written.

How it would land

  1. Engine: undoOf(row, move), called by the drafter and the sheet alike.
  2. Counter Admin: History and the Undo sheet, through /all/maker/inputs/ledger-amend.
  3. Site orders: the fix event wired to it.
  4. Tests: each kind proved red; s267 as the fixture.
